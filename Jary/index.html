创刊社论Founding editorial

为什么是 JARY：单一个体研究的必要性Why JARY: the case for a single subject

行为不是孤立事件，而是习惯、语言、环境与网络符号共同构成的连续系统。本刊将长期记录这一系统，不预设结论，也不遗漏微小变化。 Behavior is not an isolated event, but a continuous system of habit, language, environment, and internet symbolism. The journal records that system over time without predetermined conclusions.

关于本刊About the journal

《JARY行为研究》（JARY Behavior Research）创刊于2026年9月21日，是一份专注于单一特定个体（Subject JARY）的深度行为学学术期刊。本刊致力于对Jary的行为模式、认知逻辑及社会互动进行全方位的科学解构。

JARY Behavior Research, established on September 21, 2026, is a specialized academic journal dedicated to the in-depth behavioral study of a single specific subject (Subject JARY). The journal is committed to a comprehensive scientific deconstruction of Jary's behavioral patterns, cognitive logic, and social interactions.
