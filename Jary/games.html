游戏厅Game room

本刊除了严肃记录，也允许自己玩一会儿。以下小游戏都以 Subject JARY 为主角，画风是卡通校园——这不是研究数据，请勿引用。 Between rigorous records, the journal allows itself a recess. Every game below stars Subject JARY in a cartoon campus setting. None of it is research data; please do not cite it.

    JARY 逃离奶龙JARY Escapes Nailong

    奶龙在身后穷追不舍。跳过课桌、书堆与垃圾桶，滑铲躲开贴着地皮飞来的纸飞机，顺手捡走小红花把距离重新拉开。 Nailong is right behind you. Vault the desks, book piles and bins, slide under the paper planes, and grab the little red flowers to open up the gap again.

    2D RUNNER · 键盘 / 触屏Keyboard / touch

    JARY 的课间十分钟JARY's Ten-Minute Break

    在打铃之前穿过整条走廊，把散落的小红花一次捡齐。关卡设计还在纸上。 Cross the whole corridor and collect every stray red flower before the bell rings. Still on paper.
