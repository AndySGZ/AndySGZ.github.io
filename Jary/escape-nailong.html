JARY 逃离奶龙JARY Escapes Nailong

奶龙在身后穷追不舍。跳过课桌、书堆与垃圾桶，滑铲躲开贴着地皮飞来的纸飞机，顺手捡走小红花把距离重新拉开——被追上，这一趟就结束了。越跑越快，障碍之间的缝也越来越窄，连着撞三下基本就到头了。 Nailong is right behind you. Vault the desks, book piles and bins, slide under the paper planes, and grab the little red flowers to widen the gap. Get caught, and the run is over. The pace climbs, the gaps keep shrinking, and three stumbles in a row usually finish you.

场上这两位用的是真图：JARY 和他那辆共享单车，以及紧追不舍的奶龙。被追上时会弹出一张成绩卡，旁边就是奶龙捧着肚子笑的那张图。想换画风，把 Jary/assets/game/ 下的 jary.png、nailong.png、laugh.png 换成同名文件即可（建议透明底 PNG、单张 200 KB 以内）；把 canvas 上那几个 data-*-sprite 属性删掉，就会退回模块里现画的卡通角色和纯文字的成绩卡。 The cast uses real artwork now: JARY and his shared bike, with Nailong in hot pursuit — and when it catches you, a results card pops up with Nailong laughing his head off beside it. To change the art, replace jary.png, nailong.png and laugh.png in Jary/assets/game/ with files of the same name (transparent PNGs under 200 KB work best). Remove the data-*-sprite attributes from the canvas and the hand-drawn cartoon cast plus the plain-text card take over.
